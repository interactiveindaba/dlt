Ask a stylist
How do I start an order?

Send us a message on WhatsApp or complete the fitting form above. We will reply with next steps, fabric options and a timeline for your piece.

Do you make fully custom designs?

Yes. We work from your references, occasion and preferences. Bring a mood board, a screenshot or just an idea — we will help shape it into something wearable.

How long does a custom piece take?

Lead time depends on the garment and the season. Share your date as early as possible and we will confirm a realistic timeline before any work begins.

Can you deliver outside South Africa?

Yes, DTL ships worldwide. Send us your location on WhatsApp and we will confirm the delivery options and any duties that may apply.

What if I don't know my measurements?

No problem. We will guide you through the measurements on a video call, or arrange a fitting where possible. You do not need to know your size before we speak.
